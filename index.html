Privacy Policy for Country Quiz Last updated: [DATE] Country Quiz (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a mobile quiz game. This policy explains what information is used when you play. 1. How we use information - To run single-player and multiplayer gameplay - To save your preferences and high scores on your device - To show ads (AdMob) - To keep multiplayer rooms working in real time 3. Your choices - You can turn off music/sound in settings - You can reset app data in system settings - Device advertising preferences can be managed in system settings (Limit Ad Tracking / Delete advertising ID) 7.
